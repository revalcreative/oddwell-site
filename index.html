ow. A 9–5, a family, and a habit of turning everyday frustrations into actual apps.

Oddwell is where my side quests go. I kept reading clothing labels in the store and wondering what half the fibers were, so I built Wove. I kept losing the nightly “what are we eating?” debate, so I built Cook Club.

Each app is made with care and shaped by the people who use it. I love sharing the journey along the way, from first ideas to new features, and hearing what would make everyday life a little easier.

Emily Hemphill · Founder · @emmhemphill
